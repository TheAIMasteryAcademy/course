# Episode 1 homework

Pick the level that suits you. You can stop at any of them.

## Core

Answer the five questions in [quiz.md](quiz.md). Try them before you open the answers.

## Stretch

Think of one problem from your own job or life. Decide where it sits on the map: data science, big data, machine learning or generative AI. Post it in the Episode 1 homework thread in Discussions, with one sentence on why.

## Challenge

Read lesson 1 of Microsoft's free [Data Science for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/Data-Science-For-Beginners) course. Then write down one idea from it that the video didn't cover.
