## Stretch (no code): be the model

1. Open [penguins.csv](../../../data/penguins/penguins.csv) in a spreadsheet.
2. Sort it by `flipper_length_mm`.
3. Find the flipper length where Gentoo penguins start. Write your own rule: "flipper longer than ___ mm means Gentoo".
4. Count how many penguins your rule gets wrong.

You just trained a model by hand. Post your rule and your error count in [Discussions](https://github.com/TheAIMasteryAcademy/course/discussions).

## Challenge

Open [first-model.ipynb](first-model.ipynb) in GitHub Codespaces and run it. Then change `max_depth` to 1, 3 and 5, and write down the training and test scores for each. Which one would you choose, and why?

## Think about it

Your model is 99% right on the training data and 70% right on new data. What do you think happened, and what would you try first? We come back to this in lesson 3.4.
