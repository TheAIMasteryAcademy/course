# Code of conduct

This is a place to learn. Everyone starts from zero at some point.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Ask and answer in good faith. "I don't understand" is always a fair thing to say.
- Give credit when you use someone's idea or answer.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.

## What is not allowed

- Insults, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Spam, advertising or self-promotion that does not help the discussion.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Posting homework solutions before the official solution is out, unless the person asks for them.

## What happens if someone breaks these rules

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block accounts. To report something, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or discussion.
